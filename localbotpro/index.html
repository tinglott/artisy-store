Expected CSV Import Format

📄 Standard Format (works with Outscraper & most scrapers)

name,type,city,rating,reviews,phone,website "Golden Touch Nail Salon","Nail Salon","Atlanta","2.9","23","(404) 555-0182","No website" "Prestige Auto Detail","Auto Shop","Atlanta","3.1","41","(404) 555-0243","prestigeauto.com" "Haven Wellness Spa","Wellness Spa","Miami","2.7","14","(305) 555-0198","No website"

💡 Headers must be in row 1. Website column: use "No website" if they don't have one. Score is auto-calculated from rating, reviews, and website/claimed status.

Workflow Tips
1️⃣
Scrape Your Niche
Use Outscraper or Apify to pull 50–500 businesses by city + category. Filter by low rating (under 3.5 stars).
2️⃣
Import to Finder
Upload your CSV here. LocalBotPro scores each lead automatically based on website, reviews, rating & claim status.
3️⃣
AI Outreach
Click "Generate Outreach" on any lead. Grok AI writes a personalized pitch. Copy and send via email or phone.
4️⃣
Track in Pipeline
Save hot leads to Pipeline. Update status as you move them from New → Contacted → Negotiating → Closed.
