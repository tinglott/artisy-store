# Book Nook Faceless Promotion Playbook

## Traffic path
TikTok or Pinterest → `/book-nook/?utm_source=...` → consent-based reader signup → selected reading opportunity/bonus → new-release email → live Gumroad product page.

## TikTok (manual publishing only)
Create 15–30 second vertical videos from owned book covers, licensed stock visuals, animated typography, and licensed stock narration or text-only captions. Never automate login, likes, comments, follows, or posting through unofficial methods.

Each teaser structure:
1. 0–2 sec: specific emotional hook.
2. 3–12 sec: one problem, question, or short authorized excerpt.
3. 13–22 sec: show the book and reader benefit without unsupported promises.
4. Final frame: “Join the Book Nook — link in bio.”

## Pinterest
Create 2:3 vertical pins (1000×1500) using owned cover art and readable typography. Use one idea per pin. Destination URLs must include UTM parameters, for example:
`https://shop.artistrystore.com/book-nook/?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=stress_less`

Recommended boards: Calmer Living Books; Faith & Reflection; Poetry for Quiet Moments; Books for Women; Creator & Business Guides.

## Reusable content set per book
- 3 TikTok scripts: question, excerpt, transformation-without-guarantee.
- 5 Pinterest pins: cover, quote, checklist, reader question, bonus invitation.
- 1 welcome-email feature.
- 1 Book Nook catalog card.

## Safety and quality gates
- Preserve manuscripts and use disposable copies for media work.
- Use only owned/licensed covers, visuals, music, fonts, and voices.
- Verify every destination and live product detail before promotion.
- Never reward positive reviews; reward verified participation only.
- Review all generated copy for unsupported medical, financial, or earnings claims.
- TikTok publishing remains human-reviewed. Pinterest uses only authorized publishing methods.
- No public storage for protected or unpublished books.

## Uploaded pipeline adaptation
Keep the script → licensed voice/text captions → owned cover motion → captions concept. Replace local GPU-only assumptions with lightweight static motion and FFmpeg when possible. Do not execute the uploaded scripts unchanged; their model, GPU, licensing, missing-config, and dependency assumptions require review first.
