What MemoryBridge Does

Designed with Love for Seniors and Their Families

Every feature built around dignity, simplicity, and the irreplaceable value of human connection.

📖

Digital Memory Book

Seniors and family members collaborate to document life stories, photos, and meaningful memories in a beautiful digital archive.

🤖

Gentle AI Companion

A warm, patient AI that engages seniors in conversations, reminiscence games, and light cognitive exercises — always at their pace.

🧩

Brain Training Games

Daily cognitive games calibrated to ability level — word puzzles, photo memory, pattern recognition, and trivia from their era.

📞

Family Video Bridge

Simplified video calling that even the least tech-savvy senior can use — one big button, family list, done.

📷

Photo Story Narration

Upload old photos and the AI helps seniors narrate the stories behind them — creating priceless family archives.

💊

Gentle Reminders

Medication reminders, appointment alerts, and daily routines — delivered with warmth, not clinical sterility.

📊

Wellness Insights

Daily mood check-ins and cognitive engagement tracking shared securely with family caregivers who want to stay informed.

🎵

Music Memory Therapy

Personalized playlists from their era — proven to reduce anxiety and spark positive memories for dementia patients.
