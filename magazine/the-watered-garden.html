The Watered Garden

From The Liberal Soul · Episode 3 in the Sacred Cycle Renewal series

By Ting Lott, RN

“And thou shalt be like a watered garden, and like a spring of water, whose waters fail not.”

— Isaiah 58:11, KJV

The fast God chooses, Isaiah says, is bread dealt to the hungry and a home opened to the overlooked. And the promise that follows is tender: water the thirsty, and you become a watered garden. Give a spring’s worth, and you become a spring that does not run dry. It isn’t giving what’s left over — it’s drawing out your soul to the hungry: your attention, your time, your compassion, along with your bread.

Tonight’s gentle question: when have you given something of yourself, and not just something you owned?

Lord, make my life a garden where others may rest, and a spring that does not fail. Amen.
