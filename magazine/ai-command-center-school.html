AI Command Center School — Now Open

A free virtual school — Lesson 1 is live now

By Ting Lott, RN

The AI Command Center School opened its doors on November 11, 2026 — a free learning center where working people learn to use AI correctly: not as a chatbot toy, but as a real advanced process inside their own trade.

Each track teaches the same discipline through a different doorway: a real problem, an AI employee who helps carry it, the process step by step, the guardrails that keep it honest, and a checklist to try it yourself. The tracks stand on Ting’s own books — small business, teachers, nursing and caregiving, lawyers and legal work, and caregivers and families.

Lesson 1 — Your First AI Employee — is free and live now, along with the “Which AI Employee Should You Hire First?” quiz. The front door is free; the complete AI Command Center package waits inside for those who want the whole system.
