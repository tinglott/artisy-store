Lottie’s Bedtime Poems

Gentle poems to wind little ones down at night

By Ting Lott, RN

Lottie is ARTISY’s mascot — and the friendly voice behind a book of gentle bedtime poems written to slow the evening down.

Lottie’s Bedtime Poems is Ting’s own collection for the last stretch of the day: short, soft rhymes for the bedtime wind-down, made to be read aloud in a low, easy voice. If your family loves story time, Lottie’s poems sit happily beside Willow Tales on the same shelf.

One poem, one deep breath, lights low. That’s the whole recipe.
