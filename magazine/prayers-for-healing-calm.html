Prayers for Healing & Calm

A free page of prayers from ARTISY, for hard nights and heavy days

By Ting Lott, RN

Some nights the words won’t come. That is exactly what this page is for.

Prayers for Healing & Calm is a free page Ting keeps open at ARTISY — a quiet place to land when the day has been heavy and the night feels long. No sign-up, no cost. Just prayers, written in the same gentle voice as his devotionals, there whenever you need them.

If tonight is more than heavy — if you are in crisis — please call or text 988 (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) any time, day or night. You matter, and help is close.
