Procurement readiness

NAICS codes to research before bidding or registering

These codes are planning references for SAM.gov, state portals, local vendor systems, and capability-statement preparation. Final code choice should be verified against the exact service, solicitation, and agency rules.

Roofing & gutters

238160 Roofing Contractors · 238170 Siding Contractors · 238390 Other Building Finishing · 236220 Commercial/Institutional Building Construction

Common work: roof repair, inspections, maintenance, disaster recovery, housing authority and facilities projects.

Education & training

611430 Professional and Management Development Training · 611420 Computer Training · 611519 Other Technical and Trade Schools · 611710 Educational Support Services

Common work: staff training, e-learning, curriculum, LMS content, workforce preparation, compliance education.

Healthcare & health IT

621111 Offices of Physicians · 621999 Miscellaneous Ambulatory Health Care · 621210 Dental Offices · 541512/541519 Health IT and computer services

Common work: staffing, telehealth support, remote monitoring, occupational health, diagnostics, EHR-related services.

Digital products & IT

541511 Custom Software Development · 541512 Systems Design · 541519 Other Computer Services · 518210 Data Processing, Hosting, and Related Services

Common work: internal tools, software builds, cyber support, cloud migration, hosting, and digital infrastructure.
