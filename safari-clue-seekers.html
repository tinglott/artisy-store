🦁 Discover African Animals

🦓

Zebra

Striped beauty of the savanna

Zebras have unique stripe patterns, just like human fingerprints! No two zebras have the exact same stripes.
🦒

Giraffe

Tallest animal on Earth

Giraffes can grow up to 18 feet tall! Their long tongues (up to 20 inches) help them pick leaves from tall trees.
🦁

Lion

King of the jungle

Male lions are the only cats with manes! A lion's roar is so loud it can be heard from 5 miles away.
🐘

Elephant

Intelligent giant

Elephants have amazing memories and can remember other elephants for their entire lives. They use their trunks for over 40,000 different tasks!
🦁

Cheetah

Fastest land animal

Cheetahs can run up to 70 mph! Unlike other big cats, they can't roar - they chirp, purr, and growl instead.
🦛

Hippopotamus

River giant

Hippos can hold their breath for up to 5 minutes! Despite their size, they can run up to 30 mph on land.
