# Muay Thai Mastery Unleashed — Visual Technique Course (hosted)

The hosted companion to the book *Muay Thai Mastery Unleashed* by T. Lott. Open `index.html` in any web browser (phone, tablet, or computer). Everything on the course page works offline except the Spring Sia video library at the end, which links out to YouTube.

## What's inside

- **index.html** — the course: 8 lessons, 11 labeled diagrams, the 6-step method for every movement, a 4-week home practice plan, one assessment checkpoint per lesson, and a full glossary.
- **academy.html** — the Learning Academy: 10 lessons, the Spring Sia playlist embedded, 12 combination drills, a 12-question quiz with explained answers, a 23-term glossary, and a round timer. The lessons, quiz, drills, glossary and timer work offline; the video playlist needs internet. Opens from the course page, with a link back.
- **Three course videos** (play from this same folder):
  - `muay-thai-course-video.mp4` (7:54) — Course video 1: the course in 4 parts.
  - `muay-thai-mastery-unleashed-openelms.mp4` (15:32) — Course video 2: the full Open eLMS course.
  - `muay-thai-technique-training-cultural-fundamentals.mp4` (16:25) — Course video 3: the technique deep-dive.
- **Three transcript files** (.txt) — full text of each video, linked from the course page.
- **the-anatomy-of-muay-thai.pdf** (21 slides) — The Anatomy of Muay Thai: a visual field guide to the Art of Eight Limbs (the fighter's triad, the 8 weapons and their contact points, the elbow's combat space and cardinal rule).
- **cover.jpg** — the book cover.
- **Spring Sia video library** — inside the course page: 48 follow-along classes on YouTube (about 15½ hours), organized by topic. These are HER videos, linked here as referrals — tap a title to watch it on YouTube. They are not copied into this course. Needs internet. Three classes are members-only and need Spring Sia's channel membership.

## Safety first

Learn from a qualified coach. Spar only with supervision and proper gear. Never drill at full power without a coach. Stop when you are hurt. This course teaches; it does not promise fighting skill, fitness results, or self-defense outcomes. Train with a qualified coach.
