The framework

Seven gates before growth.

A good-looking nightly rate is only one part of a durable STR plan. Move in order, document assumptions, and stop when a gate does not clear.

GATE 1

Market & legal

Record demand drivers, full-year comparable ADR/occupancy, saturation, city and county rules, HOA or lease restrictions, permits and tax responsibilities.

GATE 2

Deal underwriting

Model revenue, operating costs, cash invested, debt service, NOI, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, and approximate DSCR.

GATE 3

Downside scenario

Reduce occupancy and ADR; increase operating expenses. A result is an estimate, not a promise, and negative cash flow deserves a pause.

GATE 4

Financing fit

Compare lender-specific terms, reserves, loan structure, and exit options. Verify all figures directly with a lender and qualified adviser.

GATE 5

Guest-ready setup

Budget furnishing, photography, insurance, permits, Wi-Fi, self check-in, cleaning, maintenance, supplies, and a house manual.

GATE 6–7

Operate & review

Use a pricing cadence, guest-message standards, cleaner QA, bookkeeping, inventory logs, and a monthly performance review before expanding.

Operating cadence

Review ADR, occupancy, RevPAR, gross revenue, operating costs, guest feedback, cleaning quality, repairs, and local rule changes monthly. A calendar that looks busy is not the same as profitable operations.

Team & finance basics

Keep supplier contacts, a repair reserve, invoices, tax records, guest-message templates, pricing rules, and an escalation plan. Consider self-management versus a co-host or manager only after comparing the full cost and service tradeoffs.

Required due diligence

Local rules are a gate, not an afterthought.

Verify before you rely on a model

  • City and county rules, zoning, permits, caps and minimum stays
  • Owner-occupancy, inspection, parking, noise and safety requirements
  • HOA/condo association or lease restrictions
  • Pending legislation and rule-change risk
  • Lodging/transient-occupancy tax responsibilities
  • Insurance that covers disclosed short-term rental activity

Use this tool responsibly

Staywise STR gives educational calculations from the assumptions you enter. It does not evaluate a property, approve financing, provide legal/tax/insurance advice, or guarantee income, occupancy, appreciation, approvals, or returns.

Before acting, consult qualified local counsel, a CPA/tax professional, licensed insurance professional, lender, and applicable city/county or association sources.

Quick glossary

ADRAverage daily rate: average revenue per booked night.
OccupancyShare of available nights booked over a period.
RevPARRevenue per available night: ADR × occupancy.
NOIRevenue less operating expenses, before debt service.
Cap rateNOI ÷ all-in cost basis in this tool.
DSCRApproximate NOI ÷ annual debt service; lenders use their own definitions.
