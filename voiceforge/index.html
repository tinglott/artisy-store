How to Play

Start with the goal, take one turn at a time, and use the restart button whenever your family wants a fresh round. Each game also shows these same instructions when it opens.

Your local play stats

Arcade Info

Neon Arcade is a self-contained family arcade. It works offline after this file is saved.

Fair play

Take turns, cheer for good moves, and use the restart button when you want a fresh round. Games are local pass-and-play or solo challenges.

Accessibility

Buttons have labels, strong focus rings, readable contrast, and large touch targets. Keyboard players can use tab, arrows, Enter, and Space.

What is simplified?

Chess and Checkers use friendly starter rules. Chess omits check, castling, and en-passant. Checkers allows diagonal moves and jumps, with king promotion.

Privacy

Only game totals and best scores are stored in this browser's local storage. There are no ads, purchases, accounts, or online multiplayer features.
