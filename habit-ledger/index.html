      Diagnose the need

      Every habit meets a real need. Name the habit you want to change, what it's actually giving you, and a replacement that meets the same need.

      For the difficult days

      On the days you're depleted, the goal isn't the full habit — it's a version so small it's nearly impossible to skip.

        Monthly review

        Once a month, look at the system itself — not just whether you kept it.

        Where did the environment work against you this month?

        Which lapses were about the habit, and which were about the need behind it?

        What's the smallest next version of this habit worth trying?

        Long-term success rules

        1. Make the desired behavior easy.
        2. Make the unwanted behavior inconvenient.
        3. Focus on the next choice, not a perfect streak.
        4. Address the underlying need, not just the visible habit.
        5. Use small actions on difficult days.
        6. Expect lapses and prepare for them.
        7. Review your system every month.

        The goal isn't to stop a bad habit — it's to build an environment, routine, and self-image that make the healthier choice increasingly natural.
