CarouselStudio

Transform your art into AI-powered video carousels — completely free

✨ Free Forever — No Login, No Backend

Create gallery-style showcase videos with free AI tools

Upload your art, generate smooth zoom-pan animations using HuggingFace's free text-to-video Spaces, stitch clips together with free CapCut, and share to any platform. Perfect for portfolios, products, and storytelling.

1️⃣
Gather Your Art
Use FLUX.1 Schnell via Pollinations to generate 4–6 finished art pieces. Save to your phone or device.
2️⃣
Generate Animations
Feed each artwork into a free HuggingFace Space (Wan 2.2 5B or LTX-2). Use pre-written motion prompts. Get 3–5 second animated clips back.
3️⃣
Chain for Continuity
For seamless transitions, feed the last frame of clip 1 as the starting image for clip 2. Keeps the visual thread alive across pieces.
4️⃣
Stitch & Enhance
Download all clips to your phone. Use free CapCut to add crossfades, transitions, music, and branding (logo, title card).
5️⃣
Export & Share
Export as 9:16 (vertical) for Instagram/TikTok, 1:1 for feed posts, or 16:9 for YouTube. Upload to your store, social channels, or portfolio.
💡
Pro Tip
Save your animation prompts and art themes. Reuse them for new carousels. Build a library of free, repeatable templates.
