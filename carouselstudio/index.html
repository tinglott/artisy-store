    How to Play

    1. Setup: Add 2–6 swimmers. Choose a gentle Race or competitive Survival game.
    2. Secret shark: Every player privately peeks. One player is the Shark for this round.
    3. Spin & move: Spin 1–4, travel around the reef, and land on special spaces.
    4. Attack: The Shark may tag a nearby swimmer once each round, except on a Lifeguard Tower.
    5. Win: Race—first full lap. Survival—last active swimmer.
    A free shared-device activity for family play • Ages 6+ • No accounts or personal information needed.
