# Artisty Store + Sacred Cycles classification map

**Baseline:** `store.html` at commit/source SHA `439506e59249aa9f8fac8d92de4b7f5fc432dcb6` (retrieved July 11, 2026).

## Retain on Artisty Store

These remain consumer-facing or broadly useful and should not be removed:

- Wellness and healing guides
- Romance novels and audiobooks
- Adult and Bible coloring books
- Kids Zone and family resources
- Spiritual and mindfulness guides
- Free Wellness Hub, therapist resources, Music Hub, and other free tools
- Creator resources intended for individual creators
- General digital products for individuals and families

## Sacred Cycles candidates

Move or mirror only after individual product, source, rights, cover, delivery, and listing verification:

- Enterprise Business Suite
- Success OSS — Email Campaign Manager
- Invoice Variance AI
- NovaCal AI professional/team offers
- AgentForge organization, franchise, agency, implementation, and consulting offers
- Clearly organizational licensing, workshops, group access, or workplace-program materials

## Shared or review-required

- Beyond Productivity and other broadly applicable wellbeing products
- Family Life AI and educational resources
- Creator & Business Tools where the intended buyer is unclear
- Products whose current descriptions contain unsupported claims or audience-specific wording

## Safety rules

1. Preserve the complete consumer storefront and original links before edits.
2. Do not delete or hide consumer resources merely to narrow Sacred Cycles.
3. Use minimal, reversible changes and retain the source baseline.
4. Verify every product's actual files, rights, cover, delivery, and listing before promotion.
5. Artisty Store links organizations to Sacred Cycles; Sacred Cycles links individuals and families to Artisty Store.
6. No claim of custom-domain deployment is made until the public custom-domain response shows the intended B2B page.
