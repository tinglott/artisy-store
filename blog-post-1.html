What Is the Sacred Cycles Renewal Workbook? Inside the 5 Healing Modalities

By T. Lott, RN · ARTISY Wellness · ← All Posts

If you've searched for a "sacred cycles renewal workbook," you're probably already past the point of wanting generic affirmations or a 7-day challenge that fizzles out. You want something structured enough to actually follow, and grounded enough to trust.

That's exactly the gap the Sacred Cycles Renewal Workbook was built to close.

Why "cycles," not "steps"

Most wellness workbooks are linear — day 1, day 2, day 3, finished. But healing rarely moves in a straight line. It moves in cycles: you return to the same wound from a different angle, with a little more capacity each time. The workbook is structured around that reality instead of fighting it, which is why it's organized into five healing modalities you can move through in any order, and return to as many times as you need.

The five modalities

Breathwork. The entry point for most people, because it requires no equipment and works on the nervous system directly. The workbook walks through specific breath patterns matched to specific states — grounding, releasing, energizing — rather than one generic technique for everything.

Sound therapy. Frequency and vibration as a regulation tool, not background noise. This section explains what's actually happening physiologically when sound shifts your state, so it doesn't feel like a leap of faith.

Crystal healing. Presented as a focusing ritual and intention-setting practice, with plain explanations of how different stones are traditionally used — accessible whether this is new to you or already part of your practice.

Acupressure. Specific points mapped to specific concerns (sleep, anxiety, digestion, focus), with simple diagrams so you're not guessing where to press.

Nature-rhythm practices. Reconnecting daily routines to natural cycles — light, seasons, moon phases — as a way to regulate energy without forcing it.

Who this is actually for

This isn't a workbook for someone who wants to feel like they're "doing wellness." It's for someone who's tried the surface-level stuff, didn't get lasting results, and wants a structured, repeatable practice they can return to for years — not just one season.

It's also built by someone who spent over two decades as a registered nurse before stepping into holistic wellness work, which means the physiological explanations throughout aren't guesses. They're grounded in actual clinical understanding of how the body responds to stress, breath, and rhythm.

Getting started

You don't need to do all five modalities at once. Start with the one that feels most accessible to you right now, work through it fully, and let the workbook guide you to the next cycle when you're ready.
