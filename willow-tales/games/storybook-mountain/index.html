Story Hall Complete!

Every book in the lodge is whole again. But the best page of all is the last one — the Scrambler's own story:

🌪️ The Scrambler: “I never meant to be a bother. I am only a little whirlwind, and nobody ever read TO me. I shook the books just to hear their pages rustle — it sounded almost like a story.”

🦉 Lottie: “Then that is exactly what we shall fix. Leo, Nia — one story, every night, read out loud to our friend.”

📖 And so they did. The Scrambler curled up small in the Listening Chair, the pages turned soft and slow, and for the first time ever, the little whirlwind fell fast asleep — smiling — before the story even ended. The End. (And also the beginning.)
