Procurement-readiness guide

Choose a lane before you register.

NAICS codes help describe a business in procurement systems. They are starting points, not proof of eligibility or a promise of contract awards. Verify the current code, size standard, scope, and certification rules in SAM.gov and the relevant state or local portal.

ROOF / GUTTERS

Construction trades

238160 Roofing Contractors · 238170 Siding Contractors · 238390 Other Building Finishing Contractors · 236220 Commercial and Institutional Building Construction

Typical lane: repair, replacement, inspection, facilities maintenance, and storm recovery. Local work may be listed outside SAM.gov.
LEARN / TRAIN

Education & training

611430 Professional and Management Development Training · 611420 Computer Training · 611519 Other Technical and Trade Schools · 611710 Educational Support Services

Typical lane: professional development, workforce preparation, curriculum, and e-learning.
CARE / SYSTEMS

Healthcare & health IT

621111 Offices of Physicians · 621999 Miscellaneous Ambulatory Health Care Services · 621210 Offices of Dentists · 541512 Computer Systems Design · 541519 Other Computer Related Services

Healthcare delivery requires qualified, licensed providers and applicable compliance. ARTISY presents this as business-code orientation only.
BUILD / DIGITAL

Digital products & IT

541511 Custom Computer Programming · 541512 Computer Systems Design · 541519 Other Computer Related Services · 518210 Computing Infrastructure Providers, Data Processing, Web Hosting

Typical lane: custom software, cybersecurity support, hosting, data processing, and cloud migration.

Information shown for educational planning, not legal, tax, medical, certification, or procurement advice. Confirm codes and eligibility with official sources before submitting registrations or bids.
