What Readers Will Love

Ivy Blackwood is a necromancer living in a haunted estate, trying to keep her life small and manageable. She has restrictions, rules, and a very sensible plan. Then Bea—her meddling ghost grandmother—decides the best cure for Ivy's isolation is an inconvenient human.

Callum just wants to be left alone. He's not interested in magic, chaos, or complications. When a wayward spell pulls him into Ivy's world, he discovers that peace is overrated, and connection might be worth the risk.

Bea is having the time of her afterlife orchestrating their meet-cute, interfering at every turn, and reminding everyone that the best magic is love. Even if her interference creates twice as many problems.
