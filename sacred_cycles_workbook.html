Welcome

A Letter From T. Lott, RN


Beautiful soul,

This workbook is your companion through eight weeks of intentional, guided healing. I created it for you — for every nurse, caregiver, and woman over forty who has poured herself out for others and is finally, bravely, choosing to pour back into herself.

I know what burnout feels like from the inside. I've lived it. I've sat in hospital break rooms at 3 AM wondering if this was really what I was made for. And I've found my way back — not once, but again and again, through the practices you're going to learn in this course.

This workbook is designed to be used, not just read. Write in it. Dog-ear the pages. Spill your coffee on it. The more marked up it gets, the more it's working.

Each week has space for your practice notes, your daily check-in grid, and your journal reflections. At the back, you'll find your 90-Day Wellness Planner — your road map for the months ahead.

You are not starting over. You are starting from everything you already know, and adding the tools you've been missing.

With love and 23 years of showing up,

T. Lott, RN

WEEK 1 OF 8

Foundation — Setting Your Sacred Space & Baseline Assessment

By the end of this week, you will have a designated healing sanctuary, a daily journaling habit, and your first intentional breath practice in place.

Key Techniques This Week
  • Sacred Space creation: designate your corner, clear clutter, add meaningful objects
  • Baseline Assessment journaling: body, emotions, energy — 10 minutes daily
  • Morning breath awareness: 5 minutes of conscious breathing before checking your phone
  • Evening reflection: one thing noticed, one gratitude, one release
My Practice Notes

What I'm doing this week:

Morning: Go to your sacred space. Three deep breaths (in 4, hold 4, out 6). Write your baseline.
Set one daily intention — not a to-do, an intention.
Evening: Return to your space. Write what you noticed, what you're grateful for, what you're releasing.

WEEK 2 OF 8

The Breath of Life — Breathwork Fundamentals

By the end of this week, you will be able to shift from stress response to healing response in under two minutes, anywhere, anytime.

Key Techniques This Week
  • Diaphragmatic (belly) breathing: hand on belly, breathe into expansion — your nervous system's reset
  • 4-7-8 Technique: inhale 4 counts, hold 7, exhale 8 — for acute stress and pre-sleep
  • Box Breathing: inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4 — for focus and emotional regulation
My Practice Notes

What I'm doing this week:

Morning: 5 min diaphragmatic breathing + 4 rounds of 4-7-8
Midday Emergency Reset: 3 rounds of 4-7-8 whenever stress rises
Evening: 4 rounds of Box Breathing to separate the day from the night

WEEK 4 OF 8

The Power of Sound — 432 Hz, 528 Hz, Solfeggio Frequencies

By the end of this week, you will have a 15-minute daily sound practice that deepens all other healing and measurably improves your sleep.

Key Techniques This Week
  • 432 Hz: profound calm, relaxation, natural harmony — for general daily use
  • 528 Hz ('Love Frequency'): emotional healing, transformation — for processing heavy emotions
  • Binaural Beats: Alpha (8-12 Hz) for focused calm; Delta (1-4 Hz) for deep sleep induction
  • Solfeggio Scale: 396 Hz (release fear), 417 Hz (change), 639 Hz (connection)
My Practice Notes

What I'm doing this week:

Daily: 15-minute sound session — lie down, headphones on, eyes closed. Simply receive.
Experiment: Try 396 Hz one evening. Before pressing play, ask: 'What fear am I carrying today?'
Sleep protocol: Delta binaural beats when you cannot settle. Use headphones.

WEEK 5 OF 8

Lymphatic Flow — Therapeutic Self-Massage

By the end of this week, you will have a complete personalized massage sequence with essential oil blend that reduces physical tension, supports your immune system, and teaches your body what care feels like.

Key Techniques This Week
  • Lymphatic drainage sequence: neck → shoulders → arms → chest → abdomen → legs (always upward)
  • Essential oil blends: Calm (lavender+chamomile+frankincense) / Focus (rosemary+peppermint+lemon) / Sleep (lavender+cedarwood+vetiver)
  • Always dilute: 2-3 drops essential oil per 1 tsp carrier oil (jojoba or sweet almond)
My Practice Notes

What I'm doing this week:

Morning (10 min): 5 min diaphragmatic breathing first, then apply oil blend, then lymphatic sequence
Focus extra time on your personal tension areas — neck/shoulders, legs/feet, whatever your body calls for
Evening: Sleep Blend massage on feet, neck, and temples as your goodnight ritual

WEEK 6 OF 8

Acupressure — Your 10 Meridian Points

By the end of this week, you will have targeted acupressure sequences for digestion, sleep, and energy — and you will never again be without a healing tool.

Key Techniques This Week
  • LI4 (headache/pain), ST36 (digestion/energy), CV6 (bloating), HT7 (sleep/anxiety), Yintang (calm/tension)
  • GB21 (neck/shoulder tension), LI10 (energy boost), KD3 (restoration), CV17 (heart/grief), GV20 (clarity)
  • Application: firm, comfortable pressure 1-2 min per point; exhale slowly with each press
My Practice Notes

What I'm doing this week:

Digestion: LI4 → ST36 → CV6 (1-2 min each, after meals or when bloated)
Sleep: HT7 → Yintang → KD3 (1-2 min each, before bed)
Energy: LI10 → GV20 → ST36 (1-2 min each, afternoon slump)

Integration — Building Your Daily Micro-Cycle

By the end of this week, you will have your personalized Morning Anchor, Midday Reset, and Evening Release — a sustainable daily practice designed around your real life.

Key Techniques This Week
  • Morning Anchor (5-30 min): Breathwork + crystal + intention + 2-sentence journal
  • Midday Reset (2-5 min anywhere): LI4 acupressure + 3 rounds 4-7-8 + shoulder rolls
