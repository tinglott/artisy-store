About ARTISY

A wellness studio dedicated to peace, faith, and authentic healing for the soul.

Our Story

ARTISY was born from a simple belief: that everyone deserves access to calm, healing, and peace—without complication or pretense.

Created by Ting Lott, a Registered Nurse with 13 years of night-shift experience, a teacher, and a lifelong seeker of wellness, ARTISY brings together practical wisdom, faith-rooted insights, and genuine human connection.

After years of caring for others and witnessing the deep hunger for authentic peace and spiritual grounding, we created ARTISY to offer the same compassionate, evidence-based, and soul-nourishing content we wished existed when we needed it most.

Today, ARTISY serves thousands of people seeking to live with more intention, faith, and joy.

Our Values

Authenticity

We believe in honest, no-fluff wellness. Real stories, real struggles, real peace—not Instagram perfection.

Accessibility

Healing should be affordable. We price our products for real people, not just the wealthy.

Faith-Rooted

Spirituality is personal. We honor diverse faith traditions while celebrating the universal human need for meaning.

Evidence-Based

We blend spiritual wisdom with clinical experience and research. Emotion + evidence = transformation.

Inclusive

Wellness is for everyone—all genders, backgrounds, ages, and beliefs. We meet you where you are.

Creative

Sleep stories, apps, audiobooks, affirmations—we use storytelling and art to make healing memorable and joyful.
