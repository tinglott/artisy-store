🚗 MileShot

Snap your odometer at the start and end of a trip, or batch-import a month of photos. MileShot reads the numbers and keeps your IRS-style log.

Email opens your mail app with the log filled in — you tap Send. Long logs may be trimmed by your mail app; CSV/print carry everything.

Your trips live in this browser only. Export a CSV regularly as your backup and for tax time.

Rates used for dates after June 2026

2026 is built in: business 72.5¢ (Jan–Jun) then 76¢ (from Jul 1); medical 20.5¢ then 23.5¢; charity 14¢. Update these when the IRS publishes new rates.

This is a record-keeping aid, not tax advice. Check IRS Publication 463 or your tax pro for what you may deduct.

Photo reading (optional)

Bring your own key — it is stored only in this browser, never anywhere else. When a key is set, your odometer photo is sent to that provider so it can read the number. You always confirm the reading before anything is saved. With no key, MileShot tries on-device OCR, and typing the number always works.

Email

Where the “Email my log” button on the Summary tab addresses your log. The email itself opens in your own mail app — MileShot cannot send mail by itself.
