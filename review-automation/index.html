🗺️ Your Agency Roadmap
🎯

Week 1: Target

Pick your niche (restaurants, dentists, salons, gyms). Find 10–20 local businesses with under 50 reviews. Pull their contact info.

✉️

Week 2: Outreach

Send cold email + follow-up sequence. Offer a free 14-day trial or money-back guarantee. Book 3–5 discovery calls.

🤝

Week 3: Close

Use the call scripts below. Present tiered pricing. Aim for Starter or Growth plan. Collect first payment via Stripe or PayPal.

⚙️

Week 4: Deliver

Set up review request automation. Send weekly reports. Upsell reputation monitoring. Ask for a referral after first 5 reviews.

📈

Month 2: Scale

Hire a VA to handle outreach. Stack 5–10 clients. Raise rates for new clients. Launch referral program.

💎

Month 3+: Profit

$2,500–$5,000/month MRR with 8–15 clients. Productize delivery. Sell lifetime deals. Expand to new verticals.

📞 Script 1 — Cold Call Opener

▼
YOU: "Hi, is this [Owner Name]? Great — my name is [Your Name], I'm a local reputation consultant. I'm not trying to sell you anything today — I just noticed your business on Google and wanted to share something I spotted. Do you have 60 seconds? [Wait for yes] I see you have about [X] reviews. Most [restaurants/salons/etc] in your area with 4.5+ stars and 100+ reviews are getting 30–40% more calls than businesses like yours right now — just from Google search. I help local businesses close that gap automatically. Would it be helpful if I showed you exactly how many reviews you'd need to rank above [Competitor Name] in the next 60 days?"

💬 Script 2 — Discovery Call Questions

▼
Opening: "Thanks for taking the time today! Before I show you what we do, I'd love to learn more about your business first." Questions to ask: 1. "How are most of your new customers finding you right now?" 2. "Have you ever gotten a bad review that hurt your business? How did you handle it?" 3. "Are you happy with your Google ranking when people search for [type of business] near [city]?" 4. "If you could wave a magic wand, what would ideal online reputation look like for your business?" 5. "What would 20 new 5-star reviews mean for your business this month?" After answers: "Based on what you've told me, it sounds like reviews are both an opportunity AND a risk for you right now. Let me show you what we've done for similar businesses..."

💰 Script 3 — Pricing Presentation

▼
"So here's how we work. We have three options depending on what you need: The STARTER plan is $297/month. That gets you our automated review request system — we text your customers after every visit and guide them to leave you a review on Google. Most clients see 10–20 new reviews in the first 30 days. The GROWTH plan is $497/month. That includes everything in Starter plus we monitor all your reviews 24/7 and send you alerts. We also respond to negative reviews within 2 hours using proven templates that show Google you care. The AGENCY PRO plan is $797/month. That's our premium, done-for-you service — we handle everything and send you a monthly reputation report showing your ranking improvement. Which of those sounds most aligned with what you're trying to accomplish?" [If price objection]: "I totally get it. Think of it this way — if we help you get even 5 new customers a month who each spend $100 with you, that's already $500 in new revenue. This pays for itself. And we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee."

🎯 Script 4 — Closing & Objection Handling

▼
CLOSING LINE: "So based on everything you told me, it sounds like the Growth plan is the right fit. I can get you set up today — should we go ahead?" OBJECTIONS: "I need to think about it." → "Absolutely. What specifically would help you make this decision? Is it the price, or is there something else holding you back?" "We already have someone handling this." → "That's great! Can I ask — are they getting you consistent reviews every week? Most systems set it and forget it. Ours is active and monitored daily." "This isn't a priority right now." → "I hear you. Can I ask — when a potential customer Googles you tonight and sees a competitor with 200 more reviews, is THAT a priority? We can start with our entry-level plan just to protect what you've built." "Send me more info." → "Of course! I'll send it right now — but before I do, what's the #1 thing you'd need to see in that info to move forward?"
