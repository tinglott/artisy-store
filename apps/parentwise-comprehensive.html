Quick Reference Videos

Trusted voices for the journey — from the American Academy of Pediatrics, children's hospitals, and licensed specialists. Watch when you need it; every video here was hand-picked for ParentWise families.

🍼 Ages 0–1 — Starting Out

5 Safe Sleep Essentials for Your Baby (American Academy of Pediatrics) — Watch when: setting up your baby's sleep space.

Starting Solids: Purees vs Baby-Led Weaning (PedsDocTalk, Dr. Mona Amin) — Watch when: starting solids, around 6 months.

A Pediatrician's Guide to Your Baby's Developmental Milestones (Dr. Leticia Dirks) — Watch when: wondering what's next in the first year.

🧸 Ages 1–3 — Big Feelings Begin

Toddler Meltdowns: The Calm-Connect-Correct Strategy (The Mentor Mom, licensed clinical social worker) — Watch when: tantrums hit. Chaptered, so you can jump to the part you need.

🌱 Ages 0–5 — Foundations

How a Child's Brain Develops Through Early Experiences (Brain Builders) — Watch when: you want the "why" behind everything else in ParentWise.

Sparks: Parenting Support from Birth to Age 5 (Mount Sinai Parenting Center) — Watch when: a checkup is coming up.

📱 Ages 2–5 — Screens Enter the Picture

How to Choose Content for Children Under 5 (Internet Matters expert panel) — Watch when: choosing shows and apps, and setting screen habits early.

🎓 Ages 13–18 — The Teen Years

NIMH — Mental Health Minute: Stress & Anxiety in Adolescents — Watch when: your teen seems stressed or anxious. A 60-second brief from the National Institute of Mental Health on what is happening and how to help.
