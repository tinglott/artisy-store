Family privacy

This game works in the browser and stores only score progress on this device. It does not ask for names, photos, email, location, or an account.

Safari Clue Seekers is a learning game. Animal facts are short, age-appropriate prompts for further discovery.

How to play

Start a safari. Read the animal clue, then select the animal that matches it. A correct choice earns points and shows a fun fact. Use Tab and Enter/Space to play with a keyboard.
