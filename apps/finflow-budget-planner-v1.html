Privacy, storage & limits

FinFlow is offline-first: it runs from these files and does not make network requests. Your entries are saved in this browser using localStorage, under the key finflowBudgetPlannerV1.

Limits: browser storage can be erased when you clear browser/site data, use private browsing, switch browsers/devices, or if storage is unavailable. It does not sync, encrypt, or automatically back up your data. Export JSON backups regularly and keep them securely.

No financial accounts: FinFlow has no bank connection, no Plaid integration, no bank branding, and no account login or credential collection. It is an organizational tool—not financial, tax, legal, investment, or credit advice.
