What's inside ParentWise Complete

Eight tools in one place, organized by the CALM tabs so the right help is one tap away — even mid-meltdown (yours or theirs).

The Big Red SOS Calm Minute

When a moment is about to boil over, press the big red button and breathe through a guided 60-second reset — for you first, so you can steady the room.

Routine Builder

Build the day's routines step by step, so mornings, homework, and bedtime run on a plan instead of on reminders and raised voices.

First / Then Board

A simple two-step promise your child can see: first this, then that. It turns "because I said so" into a picture that makes sense.

Focus Timer

A gentle visual timer that makes time visible — homework, chores, and quiet time get a clear start and a clear finish.

Visual Schedule

The whole day laid out in pictures and steps your child can check off — fewer surprises, fewer battles over what comes next.

Token Board

Reward progress you can both see. Tokens earned for the small wins add up to the celebration — positive, concrete, and fair.

Night Mode

A softer, dimmer view for winding down — bedtime routines and late-night moments without the bright-screen jolt.

CALM Tabs

Everything is sorted under calm, clear tabs, so in a heated moment you're not hunting through menus — the tool you need is right there.
