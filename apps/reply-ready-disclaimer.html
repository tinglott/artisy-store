What this product is — and is not

ReplyReady is a one-page app that gives you 3 ready-to-send replies for any customer message, in under 5 minutes, plus a 7-day plan to clear your message backlog. It gives you the words — you copy one and send it yourself. It does not answer messages for you, it is not a subscription, and it is not a promise of any outcome beyond the replies and the plan themselves.
