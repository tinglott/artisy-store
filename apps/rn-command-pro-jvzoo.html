RN Command Pro — Nurse Command Center

One app that replaces the sticky notes, the calculator app, the reference binder, and the telehealth scramble. Designed by Ting Lott, RN — 17 years of nursing, from behavioral health to home care.

What's inside the Professional Bundle

  • Clinical documentation — quick templates for shift handoff, SBAR, psych / mental status, vitals, and neuro checks. Dictate by voice, save memos on your own device, and back everything up to a single file with one tap.
  • Transcription Studio — record a voice note or upload an audio file; Whisper transcribes it 100% on your device. No audio is uploaded anywhere. (First use downloads the speech model once.)
  • Telehealth visits — create an appointment and get a private video-visit link (Jitsi) to send your patient; they join from any browser, no account or app install. With patient consent, live transcription flows straight into your visit note.
  • 8 clinical calculators — mcg/kg/min to mL/hr, narcotic waste, liquid and tablet dosing, I&O balance, BMI, O2 tank time, ideal body weight. High-risk calculations require a dual-verification check.
  • OASIS-E2 guided assessment — the complete CMS OASIS-E2 (effective April 1, 2026), researched from CMS sources; the form shows only the items each assessment reason requires, and BIMS, CAM, and PHQ scores calculate as you go.
  • Hospice — "Painting the Picture" — visit documentation built to show decline measurably: PPS, Karnofsky, FAST, and NYHA scales, HOPE visit types, and recertification comparison built in.
  • Behavioral-health toolkit — Mental Status Exam, the Columbia (C-SSRS) suicide risk screener, PHQ-9 and GAD-7 with live totals, CIWA-Ar and COWS withdrawal scoring, the AIMS movement scale, and a structured safety plan.
  • Wound care — full wound documentation plus the Braden Scale and the PUSH Tool, scored automatically.
  • MDS Companion, Dementia Care Companion, School Nurse Visit Log, and Dialysis documentation — the key items each setting tracks, including the Zarit caregiver burden scale, a home safety checklist, an ABC behavior log, and automatic dialysis fluid math.
  • Critical-care rapid reference — Glasgow Coma Scale, burn Rule of 9s, the H's & T's, and ACLS drug cards.
  • Built-in nursing knowledge library — lab values, insulin, anticoagulation, lithium, serotonin syndrome vs. NMS, suicide risk assessment, de-escalation, isolation precautions, sepsis, and more.
  • Printable Quick Reference Cards (PDF) — only in this edition: GCS, Rule of 9s, H's & T's, ACLS drug cards, the scale list, and an SBAR outline, formatted to print and carry.
  • 12 months of updates — only in this edition: email support any time during your year and receive the current version file.
  • 10 languages — English, Español, Français, Português, Tagalog, Arabic, Hindi, Kiswahili, Kreyòl Ayisyen, and Vietnamese, with one globe switcher.

Delivery: instant download of the bundle file. Unzip, open index.html in Chrome or Edge, and start working. Your notes, memos, and appointments are stored only in your own browser — nothing is uploaded to us.
