Support — Every Gift Covered

If something is not working, or you have a question about your gift list, this page tells you how to reach us and what happens next.

What you bought

Every Gift Covered is one file that opens in your browser: your whole Christmas gift list on one screen — a holiday-number budget bar for the season and for each person, gift tracking from idea to given, idea starters for 7 hard-to-buy-for people, the 4-gift rule, the Black Friday buy-check, a 7-day finish plan, a days-left-until-December-20 counter, and print plus start-next-year-fresh reset. It is delivered as a download right after checkout through JVZoo. It opens on any phone and works without the internet once it has loaded. Your list is saved on your own device — nothing is uploaded, nothing is shared, and there is no account to make. Buy it once, use it every Christmas.

How to get help

Email us: tinglott@gmail.com — write from the email address you used at checkout if you can, and tell us what happened in a sentence or two. We answer every email ourselves.

Bring your list: the free Sacred Cycle Renewal community on Whop is where you can ask about a hard-to-buy-for person or holding your budget. The first 5 founding customers also get personal support from Ting.

I can't find my download

Your download link comes from JVZoo right after checkout. If you cannot find it, check your spam or promotions folder first, then email tinglott@gmail.com with the name and email address used at checkout and we will help you get it.

The file won't open

Every Gift Covered opens in any modern browser on your phone or computer — no app to install. If it will not open, try a different browser first, then email us and tell us which device and browser you are using.

Refunds

Refunds are handled through JVZoo within the 30-day refund period. Email tinglott@gmail.com and we will help you with it.
