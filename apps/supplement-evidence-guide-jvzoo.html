What's inside

The Supplement Evidence Guide (Newborn Solutions, a T. Lott Creative resource) covers 23 health conditions — sleep, blood sugar support, joint pain, heart and metabolic health, gut health, memory, strength, and more. Every condition card follows the same honest structure:

  • What it is — the supplements most commonly studied for the condition.
  • What research shows — a plain-language summary with an evidence grade: Strong, Moderate, Mixed, or Limited. When the evidence is weak, the card says so.
  • ⚠ Cautions & interactions — visually separate, impossible to miss: prescription interactions, kidney/liver cautions, pregnancy and surgery warnings.
  • Search the studies yourself — a live PubMed link on every card.

The guide also includes a "Before taking any supplement" safety checklist and the free public sources it is built on (NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, MedlinePlus, NCCIH), so you can verify everything in it. Search and category filters help you find your condition fast. Mobile-first, no install — it works in any modern browser, and once saved it works offline too.
