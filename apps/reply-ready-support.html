Support — ReplyReady

If something is not working, or you have a question about answering your customers, this page tells you how to reach us and what happens next.

What you bought

ReplyReady is one file that opens in your browser: 24 ready replies for any customer message (8 situations × 3 tones — warm, short, detailed), a template library, a 7-day plan to clear your message backlog, and a win tracker that counts your replies and day streak. It is delivered as a download right after checkout through JVZoo. It opens on your phone and works without the internet once it has loaded. Your shop details are saved on your own device — nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no account to make.

How to get help

Email us: tinglott@gmail.com — write from the email address you used at checkout if you can, and tell us what happened in a sentence or two. We answer every email ourselves.

Ask about a tricky message: the free AI Command Center School community on Whop is where small business owners help each other word a reply. The first 5 founding customers also get personal support from Ting.

I can't find my download

Your download link comes from JVZoo right after checkout. If you cannot find it, check your spam or promotions folder first, then email tinglott@gmail.com with the name and email address used at checkout and we will help you get it.

The file won't open

ReplyReady opens in any modern browser on your phone or computer — no app to install. If it will not open, try a different browser first, then email us and tell us which device and browser you are using.

Refunds

Refunds are handled through JVZoo within the 30-day refund period. Email tinglott@gmail.com and we will help you with it.
