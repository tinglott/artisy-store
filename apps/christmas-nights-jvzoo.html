These Stories Have Been Read at Christmas for Over a Hundred Years

The stories come from The Children's Book of Christmas Stories (1913), edited by Asa Don Dickinson and Ada M. Skinner — a collection families have loved since before your grandparents were children. The words are lightly modernized for today, the stories are the same ones: Andersen's Fir-Tree, Dickens at Fezziwig's warehouse and at the Cratchits' dinner, the shepherds on the first Christmas night. What 25 Christmas Nights adds is the plan: one story a night, a question to talk about, and a star to light — so the reading actually happens, every night, instead of being the thing December crowds out.

Questions? Here Are the Honest Answers

What exactly do I get?

25 Christmas Nights as one file that opens in your browser: all 25 stories in full (Dec 1–25), the nightly read-aloud card, the 25-night star chart with a progress bar, and a question after every story. Delivered as a download right after checkout.

Where do the stories come from?

The stories come from The Children's Book of Christmas Stories (1913), edited by Asa Don Dickinson and Ada M. Skinner, Doubleday, Page & Company — a public-domain collection, published 1913. The words are lightly modernized (old spellings fixed), the stories are the same ones families have read for over a hundred years. The nightly notes, the questions, the star chart, and the reading plan are new, written for this edition. Full source details are printed inside the app.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No account. It opens in your browser on your phone, and once it loads it works without the internet. Your stars are saved on your own device — nothing is sent anywhere.

How long is each night?

About ten minutes a night — one story read aloud, then one question to talk about. The stories average about 2,000 words each.

What if we miss a night?

The chart waits for you. Read two stories the next night, or pick up where you left off — there is no penalty, and the stars stay lit for the nights you did read. The plan is there to help, not to scold.

What is the star chart?

A chart of 25 stars, one for each night. Tap a star when you finish that night's story. The chart fills up and the progress bar moves, so your family can see how far you've come — and on Christmas morning, all 25 stars are lit.

What if I get stuck, or want to share how a night went?

Share it in the free Sacred Cycle Renewal community — other families are reading the same nights. The first 5 founding families also get personal support from Ting.

How does the founding offer work?

The first 5 founding families pay $7.49. After the 5th sale the price rises to $14.99 automatically. The first 5 also get personal support from Ting.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. $7.49 once today — the founding price for the first 5 founding families; $14.99 after the 5th sale — yours forever, and yours again every December.

What comes after the 25 nights?

When the stories are done, keep the reading going all year with Willow Tales — a calm bedtime story library for kids. And for you: Sacred Cycles, the 8-week mind-body renewal journey.
