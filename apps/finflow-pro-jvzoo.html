What FinFlow Pro does

FinFlow Pro is a budgeting and debt-planning dashboard that runs entirely in your web browser. You type in your own figures — there are no bank connections, no logins, and nothing is uploaded anywhere — and it turns them into a clear picture:

  • Net-worth tracker — your assets and your debts side by side, with your net worth calculated for you.
  • Income & expenses — record what comes in and what goes out, with spending sorted by category so you can see where the money actually goes.
  • Debt payoff planner — enter each debt's balance, APR, and minimum payment, then see a payoff plan built on the avalanche method (extra payments aimed at the highest-interest debt first, the order that costs the least interest), with a projected debt-free date and your debt-to-income ratio.
  • Insights — plain-language observations drawn from your own numbers, like what share of your income your debt payments take.
  • CSV export — download your data as a spreadsheet file any time, for your own records or a backup.

Everything is stored on your own device, in your browser. It works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet, or computer, and once it has loaded it works offline too. No account, no install, no subscription.
