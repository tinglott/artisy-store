    Financial Wellness Score A FRIENDLY GUIDE — NOT A GRADE

    One gentle number that sums up four parts of money health: your emergency cushion, your savings rate, your debt load, and how steadily you keep your money habits. It is calculated openly, from the numbers below — no judgment, and no one sees it but you. Think of it as a compass, not a report card.

    Not enough numbers yet. Add income, expenses, assets, or debts elsewhere in FinFlow Pro — or type your own numbers in the form below — and your score appears here.
    —
    out of 100
    How this is calculated. Each part is scored 0–100, then combined: emergency cushion 30%, savings rate 25%, debt load 25%, money habits 20%. A part with no data is left out and the rest are re-weighted, so the score only ever rests on real numbers.
    • Emergency cushion: the months of spending your cash & savings could cover. Six months or more earns the full 100; the score grows evenly up to six months.
    • Savings rate: the share of this month's income you kept. Keeping 20% or more earns the full 100.
    • Debt load: your monthly debt payments as a share of income. No payments due earns 100; payments at half your income or more earn 0, evenly in between.
    • Money habits: how many of the last 7 days you checked off, across all the habits you track below.

    This is a simple guide built from common rules of thumb — not a medical score, not a credit score, and not financial advice.

    Optional: type your own number for any part and the score uses yours for that part; leave a part blank and it is figured from your FinFlow entries.

    Privacy, Plainly

    FinFlow Pro stores everything you enter in your browser's local storage on this device only. Your data is never uploaded, never sent to a server, and never shared — the person who built this page can't see your numbers. There are no bank connections in this tool: no logins, no account linking, no syncing. If you clear your browser's site data, your entries are erased with it — export a CSV first if you want a backup. Use Invisible Fortress (top right) to blur every dollar amount when someone is looking over your shoulder.
