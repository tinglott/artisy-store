    Privacy, Plainly

    FinFlow Pro stores everything you enter in your browser's local storage on this device only. Your data is never uploaded, never sent to a server, and never shared — the person who built this page can't see your numbers. There are no bank connections in this tool: no logins, no account linking, no syncing. If you clear your browser's site data, your entries are erased with it — export a CSV first if you want a backup. Use Invisible Fortress (top right) to blur every dollar amount when someone is looking over your shoulder.
