# ARTISY Canva Templates - Daily Auto-Promotion (n8n Workflow)

## Workflow: Daily Rotating Social Post

**Trigger:** Cron (8 AM ET daily)

**Flow:**
1. Time trigger → Every day at 8 AM ET
2. Randomize post selection → Pick from 5 social posts
3. Format content → Adapt post to platform specs
4. Post via Content360 API
5. Log to database → Track engagement
