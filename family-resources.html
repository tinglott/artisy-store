🆘 Crisis Resources — Available 24/7

You Are Not Alone.

As a nurse, the most important thing I can do is make sure you have the right number when you need it most. Save these. Share them. They save lives.

Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

988

Call or text 988 anytime, day or night. Free, confidential, available 24/7. Also at 988lifeline.org

Crisis Text Line

Text HOME to 741741

Text-based crisis support. Free, 24/7. Great for kids and teens who can't make a call.

NAMI Helpline

1-800-950-6264

National Alliance on Mental Illness. Monday–Friday, 10am–10pm ET. Information, support, referrals.

Domestic Violence Hotline

1-800-799-7233

The National DV Hotline. 24/7, confidential, safe chat available. Also text START to 88788.

SAMHSA (Substance)

1-800-662-4357

Free treatment referral and information service for substance use disorders. 24/7, bilingual.

Caregiver Action Network

1-855-227-3640

For caregivers who are burning out. Education, peer support, and respite resources.
