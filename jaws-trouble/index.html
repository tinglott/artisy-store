      How to Play Jaws Trouble

      1. Setup: Each player gets 4 colored swimmers in their Home.
      2. Shark ritual: Each round one player is secretly Jaws. In Solo mode only you peek — the bots keep their secrets.
      3. Roll & move: Press POP! for a die (1–6). Roll a 6 to bring a swimmer out of Home onto Start, and take an extra turn. Move clockwise around the reef.
      4. Special spaces: Treasure (+shells), Current (forward), Undertow (back), Lifeguard (safe from Jaws), Ripple (event card).
      5. Bumping: Land on an opponent → send them back to Home.
      6. Jaws attack: Once per round the secret Shark may tag an adjacent swimmer not on a Lifeguard Tower → they go Home. Beware: a bot Shark strikes without warning!
      7. Win: First player to get all 4 swimmers into their Finish zone wins. Finish entry needs an exact roll — no overshooting.

      Ages ~6+. A reef-racing party game of dice, bumps and one hidden shark.
