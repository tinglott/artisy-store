You’re not falling apart. You’re in need of a prayer.

These are gentle words for the moments when life feels too heavy, written by a nurse of 17 years who has sat with people at their hardest moments. These words have been prayed in hospital rooms, in cars, and in silent moments at 3 AM. Read them slowly. Take the one you need today.

Drawn from the book Prayers of Healing: Finding Calm in Chaos — its chapters on anxiety, burnout, and heavy days. Free here, in full, whenever you need them.

“Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.”— Philippians 4:6-7 (KJV)

When anxiety takes hold

“Anxiety is a complex emotion that many adults experience, often manifesting as a sense of dread or overwhelming worry. Recognising anxiety as a natural response to these pressures is the first step in understanding its nature.”

“Through scripture and prayer, we can find solace, reminding ourselves that we are not alone in our struggles and that faith can guide us through turbulent times.”

“We can lay our burdens at His feet and trust in His unfailing love.”

“Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.”— 1 Peter 5:7 (KJV)

A quiet place to pray

“Choose a quiet and comfortable spot where you feel at peace. It could be a corner of your living room, a garden, or even a dedicated prayer room. As you settle in, take a moment to breathe deeply, centre your thoughts, and invite the Holy Spirit to guide your prayers.”

“Meditative prayer, focusing on silence and contemplation, allows for personal introspection and connection with God. In moments of stillness, we can listen for divine guidance.”

Morning: start the day with peace

“By dedicating time to prayer and scripture, we can cultivate a sense of calm that reverberates throughout the day. This sacred practice not only centres our thoughts but also connects us deeply with the divine, fostering resilience against the chaos that life often brings.”

Evening: calm before sleep

“In the stillness of the evening, we can find a moment to pause, breathe, and reconnect with our faith. It is in this sacred space that we can release the burdens of the day and invite peace into our hearts.”

“The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.”— Psalm 34:18 (KJV)

Words to hold onto

Speak one of these slowly, and let it sit with you:

“I am loved.”
“God is with me.”
“I am surrounded by God’s love.”
“I am worthy of peace.”
“I am enough.”
“Peace surrounds me.”
“I am at peace with my past.”

Through faith, we can indeed find peace

Prayer is not about perfection but about connection. Some days will feel harder than others, and that is entirely normal. Keep coming back to the quiet place. Keep bringing your worries to God. Healing is a journey best undertaken together — and you are not walking it alone.
