Complete Guide to Sacred Cycle Tracking

By Ting Lott • June 8, 2026 • 8 min read

Your sacred cycle is one of the most powerful tools for health, energy, and spiritual alignment. In this guide, I'll share exactly how to track it, why it matters, and how to leverage each phase for maximum wellbeing.

What Is Your Sacred Cycle?

Your sacred cycle is your menstrual cycle—a 28-day rhythmic pattern that influences your physical energy, emotional capacity, creativity, and spiritual receptivity. Far from being something to manage, it's a gift to master.

As a registered nurse with 5+ years of healthcare experience, I've seen firsthand how women who understand their cycles experience better health outcomes, higher energy, and deeper spiritual connection.

The 4 Phases Explained

Phase 1: Menstruation (Days 1-5)

Energy Signature: Introspective, grounding, releasing

  • Rest and restore—this is your inner winter
  • Journaling and meditation are powerful
  • Physical activity: gentle yoga, walking, stretching
  • Spiritually: release what no longer serves you

Phase 2: Follicular (Days 6-13)

Energy Signature: Rising energy, optimism, social

  • Start new projects and initiatives
  • Ideal for learning, planning, and brainstorming
  • Physical activity: strength training, cardio, intensity
  • Spiritually: manifest and set intentions

Phase 3: Ovulation (Days 14-20)

Energy Signature: Peak confidence, magnetic, expressive

  • Your superpower phase—maximum charisma and energy
  • Best for public speaking, sales, negotiations, visibility
  • Physical activity: high intensity, competitive activities
  • Spiritually: magnetic manifestation period

Phase 4: Luteal (Days 21-28)

Energy Signature: Introspective again, details-focused, completing

  • Wrap up projects, organize, refine systems
  • Your "second wind" of productivity (different quality)
  • Physical activity: moderate, consistent movement
  • Spiritually: ancestor work, deep healing, processing

How to Track Your Cycle

Simple daily tracker:

  • Day 1 = first day of full menstruation
  • Note: energy level (1-10), mood, physical symptoms, dreams, intuitive hits
  • Track for 2-3 cycles to see your unique pattern
  • Apps: Flo, Natural Cycles, Clue (or use a simple Google Sheet)

Why This Matters for Success

When you work WITH your cycle instead of against it:

  • 💪 Energy: 40% more sustainable energy throughout the month
  • 🎯 Productivity: Launch projects during follicular, complete during luteal
  • 💰 Wealth: Schedule sales calls during ovulation (peak persuasion power)
  • ✨ Spirituality: Deepen practice with phase-aligned rituals
  • ❤️ Relationships: Understand your needs and communicate them

Start Your Practice Today

You don't need expensive apps or complicated systems. Start with:

  1. Mark Day 1 of your next cycle
  2. Each day, note your energy (1-10 scale)
  3. Review after 3 cycles—you'll see clear patterns
  4. Align your work, relationships, and spiritual practice to these phases
