The Story Behind the Brand

23 Years of Holding Hands at the Bedside

T. Lott is a Registered Nurse with 23 years of experience — most of them spent in emergency rooms and ICUs watching people fight for one more day. After years of witnessing loss, burnout, addiction, and miracles, Ting started writing.

Every audiobook, every ebook, every word comes from someone who has been in the room when families got the worst news of their lives. This isn't motivational fluff. This is real talk from the front lines of human suffering and resilience.
