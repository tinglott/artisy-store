The four bedtime paths

Every child settles differently. The quiz matches yours to one of these paths — and every path lives inside the same Willow Tales library, so you can wander between them any night you like.

The Cozy Together Path

For lap-snugglers and page-turners. Stories you read side by side in the 3D flipbook, ending the day connected.

First story: Whispering Willow

The Listen & Drift Path

For tired grown-ups and audio lovers. Press play, and warm narration carries your child the whole way to sleep.

First story: Franklin of Gluntenburg

The Calm-Down Path

For wiggly bodies and racing minds. Short, gentle tales made to slow the evening down, one soft page at a time.

First story: The Robot's First Snowfall

The Story Explorers Path

For "one more chapter!" kids. Big adventures and long journeys that unfold over nights and weeks.

First story: FORE! The Legend of Algaga

Inside the Willow Tales library

Seven books plus the 12-tale Willow Tales Bedtime Stories collection, read-aloud narration, a page-turning 3D reader, and Lottie — the friendly companion who tells jokes and stories inside safe, parent-approved walls. Whichever path the quiz picks, all of this is waiting.
