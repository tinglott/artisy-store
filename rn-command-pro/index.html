Telehealth Visits

Video visits powered by Jitsi — free, no account or app needed for your patient. Each visit gets its own private room link you can copy and send.

Privacy first: use patient initials only — never full names or medical record numbers here. Anyone holding a visit link can join that room, so share links the same way you'd share a phone number. Live transcription only starts after the patient consents (checkbox below the video).

How it works

1. Create the appointment — a unique Jitsi room link is generated.
2. Tap Copy Link and text it to your patient. They tap it and join from any browser — no install, no account.
3. Tap Start Visit when it's time. Get consent, tick the box, and start live transcription so the conversation flows straight into your visit note.
4. End Visit & Save Note files it under Saved Memos in the Notes tab.
