Neurodivergent Support

ADHD Parenting Hub

Practical tools and videos for parents of toddlers with ADHD traits — and for parents who have ADHD themselves. Built from real parent experiences.

Externalize Everything

Working memory is limited. Use picture schedules, first-then boards, landing pads by the door, and phone alarms for transitions. Stop trying to hold it all in your head.

Make It Visual

Picture lists for morning routines, visual timers, and color-coded family calendars dramatically reduce daily friction for both kids and ADHD parents.

Movement & Sensory

Build movement breaks into the day. Offer fidgets, trampolines, or outdoor time before hard tasks. Sensory needs are real and valid.

Connection First

Co-regulation before correction. Your calm is the child's anchor. Repair after rupture is the real skill — not perfection.
