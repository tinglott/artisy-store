How to Use AgentForge

Step 1: Load Standard SOP

Your company starts with our standard SOP templates. These cover all 15 agents and 4 departments.

Step 2: Upload Your Protocols

Upload your company's specific SOPs, policies, and docs for each department. Agents will learn from these.

Step 3: Choose a Workflow Template

Select a pre-built workflow (Daily Standup, Lead Gen, Customer Retention, etc.) or design your own.

Step 4: Run the Workflow

Click "Run Workflow" to execute. Agents follow your SOP and coordinate with each other automatically.

Step 5: Monitor Execution

Watch the execution log in real-time. All agent actions are logged with timestamps and rationales.
