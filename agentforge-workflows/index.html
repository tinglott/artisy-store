💡 Pro Tips

  • Be specific: Include exact procedures, approval chains, and decision frameworks
  • Include examples: Good/bad examples help AI understand nuance
  • Add context: Explain WHY you do things, not just the HOW
  • Update regularly: As processes change, upload updated docs
  • Use clear formatting: Numbered lists, tables, and headers help AI parse better

  1. You upload: SOP docs, policies, past reports to Knowledge Base
  2. AI ingests: System parses PDFs/docs, extracts procedures and rules
  3. Agents customize: Each agent applies YOUR procedures to its tasks
  4. Consistency: All agents follow same company standards & terminology
  5. Continuous learning: New uploads immediately improve agent behavior
