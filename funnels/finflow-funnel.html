Questions? Here Are the Honest Answers

Does FinFlow connect to my bank?

No. You enter income, expenses, assets, and debts by hand. Nothing is pulled from a bank, and everything stays on your own device.

No. FinFlow Pro organizes and shows your numbers. It does not give financial advice and promises no returns.

Is the what-if projection a forecast?

No. It rearranges your own numbers so you can see them — it is not a prediction of what will happen.

Does it work outside the US?

FinFlow works with numbers you enter by hand, so it is not tied to any bank — but it was built around US money context, so check that the labels and currency fit how you track money where you live.
