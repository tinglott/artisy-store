Questions? Here Are the Honest Answers

Does HaulBook replace my ELD?

No. It does the paperwork the ELD never touches — everything around the ELD. It does not connect to your engine and does not replace a registered ELD, official hours-of-service records, or your carrier’s DVIR system.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. One payment of $59, on your own phone, with your records staying on your own device.

What about per-diem?

Nights away are totaled at the 2026 rate of $80/day with the 80% deductible portion estimated. That is an estimate, not tax advice.

What can I export?

Reports in CSV, print, and email — detention, mileage, money statistics, and checklists with timestamps.

How do I get it?

Instant download after checkout — one ZIP with the app file and a start-here guide. Open the app in your phone’s browser, add it to your home screen, and it runs offline.
