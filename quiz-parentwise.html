      The four pressure points

      Most parents live in one of these — and drift into a second on hard weeks. Whichever one is yours, ParentWise starts there instead of handing you a generic plan.

      Pressure point 01

      The Morning Rush

      Chaotic starts, lost shoes, and a day that's already behind before it begins. The fix is a routine your child can see — not one you have to repeat louder.

      Pressure point 02

      The Big Feelings Storm

      Meltdowns that come fast and feel enormous — for your child, and then for you. The fix is a calm-down path you've both practiced before the storm.

      Pressure point 03

      The Focus Tug-of-War

      Homework battles, instructions that don't land, follow-through that evaporates. The fix is smaller steps, visible progress, and First / Then clarity.

      Pressure point 04

      The Bedtime Battle

      Evenings that stretch for hours and leave nobody rested. The fix is a wind-down the body learns — same steps, same order, dimmer and slower.
