# Connected brand QR codes

These QR codes keep the brands separate while making the relationship clear:

- `artistystore-store.svg` — family-friendly consumer store: https://shop.artistrystore.com/store.html
- `sacred-cycles-b2b.svg` — Sacred Cycles organizational/B2B destination: https://sacredcycles.sbs/

## Brand relationship

- **Artisty Store** serves individuals and families with practical, creative, and whole-person resources.
- **Sacred Cycles** serves organizations with whole-person wellbeing programs, group resources, workshops, and partnership conversations.

They are sister brands with shared values. Sacred Cycles can point people to Artisty Store for individual products; Artisty Store can point organizations to Sacred Cycles for B2B support.

The Sacred Cycles domain is the intended B2B destination and must be publicly checked after domain connection before treating it as live.
