Privacy Policy

ARTISY / T. Lott Creative · Effective November 2, 2026

This policy describes how ARTISY ("we", "us"), operated by Ting Lott, handles information in connection with shop.artistrystore.com and our digital products.

What this website collects

This is a static website. It has no user accounts and no checkout of its own. Like most websites, the hosting provider may record standard server logs (such as pages visited and browser type) for security and reliability.

Purchases happen on the storefronts

When you buy an ARTISY product, the purchase is processed by that storefront — Gumroad, Whop, JVZoo, Etsy, or a similar platform — under its own privacy policy. Those platforms share with us the information needed to deliver your product and provide support (such as your name, email address, and what you purchased). We never see or store your full payment card details.

Email list

If you subscribe to our email list or request a free resource, we receive your email address through our email provider (Kit) and use it to send the requested material and our newsletter. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from future mailings.

The ARTISY Guide

Questions typed into the ARTISY Guide chat on this site are processed by an AI service to generate an answer about our products. Please do not enter sensitive personal or health information into the guide.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for advertising profiles, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time what purchase or subscription information we hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to tinglott@gmail.com. Storefront records are also available through the platform where you purchased.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version will always live at this page, with its effective date above.
