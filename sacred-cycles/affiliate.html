What Sacred Cycles is

Sacred Cycles Renewal: Complete Journey is a wellness platform designed by Ting Lott, RN — a psychiatric nurse of 17 years — and sold under his ARTISY brand. One purchase opens a hub of six rooms:

  • Movement — the 365-Day Yoga Journey: a full year of chair yoga and light resistance-band practice, written out day by day in four phases, beginner to advanced, with a 48-exercise library. Live in the app now: 288 short pose demonstration videos matched to the journey’s exercises — follow a pose at a time.
  • Rest — a Yoga Nidra Deep Rest library (7 chapters) inside the app.
  • Nutrition — a keto recipe video collection, opening soon.
  • Wellness — a health & wellness video collection, opening soon.
  • Mind — a self-help collection, opening soon.
  • Therapy & Support — two live supportive companions: Divine Counsel (faith-based reflection) and Monica (an AI wellness companion). They are support tools, not therapy or medical care.

Buyers pay once. There is no subscription — that own-it difference is the honest heart of the offer.

“A whole year of gentle movement — already planned for you.”

Most people don’t quit exercise because they’re lazy; they quit because every day starts with “what do I do today?” Sacred Cycles answers that question 365 times: sit down, open today’s page, follow the warm-up, the exercises, the cool-down. Chair-based, joint-friendly, made for real bodies. If your audience is seniors or beginners, lead with the plan, not the poses.
