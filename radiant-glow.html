Healthy skin for the long run — gentle routines that support your skin over time, by Ting Lott, RN

Longevity, not a fight against age.

Most skincare asks you to fight your age. Radiant Glow asks something kinder: what would keep your skin healthy, resilient and comfortable over time?

That is skin longevity — protecting skin, feeding its barrier, renewing it gently, and staying consistent. Not a quick fix. Not a promise. Honest habits, in one simple app you open in your browser.

Radiant Glow was designed by Ting Lott, a psychiatric nurse of 17 years, in the same gentle voice he has used for a career of helping people: calm, plain, and honest about what a routine can and cannot do.

  • Protect — daily sun protection, the single most evidence-backed step there is.
  • Barrier first — a gentle cleanser and a ceramide-rich moisturizer, before any active.
  • Renew gently — retinoids or a gentler alternative, slowly, plus one mild exfoliation a week.
  • Defend — vitamin C and niacinamide against daily wear.
  • Hydrate — water through the day, and moisture layered in.
  • Sleep & restore — 7–9 hours, because repair happens while you sleep.
  • Live the long game — colorful food, gentle movement, lower stress, and above all, consistency.

How it works in three steps

Skin Longevity Coach — Radiant Glow works in three simple steps:

  1. Step 1: Set your profile once — age, skin type, concerns — and read the seven-pillar longevity guide so you know why each step is there.
