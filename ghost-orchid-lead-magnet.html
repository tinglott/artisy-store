Ghost Orchid – The Phantom Bloom

Dendrophylax lindenii · Florida Cypress Swamps

Advanced 5-inch hoop Blanc / Green / Ivory palette

The Ghost Orchid grows without leaves or roots in the shadows of Florida's cypress swamps. It is seen only at night, pollinated by a single species of giant sphinx moth. Most people search for years and never find one. This embroidery design captures that elusive quality — ethereal white petals stitched as if dissolving into pale linen, present and not-present at once.
