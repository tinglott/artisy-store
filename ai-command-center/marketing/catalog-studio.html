Catalog Studio

Sample catalog: 20 real ARTISY products from Ting Lott — replace them with your own on the Products tab. Honest limits: this page tracks nothing — no views, no clicks, no analytics. Product pictures are colored tiles, not hosted photos. Buy buttons go to the seller's own checkout (Gumroad or JVZoo). Your data stays in this browser.

One product per row. Columns: name, category, price, sku, description, link — the link is the product's buy link (its real checkout page). Paste from a spreadsheet export or load a CSV file, then apply. Your data stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded.

What Catalogy does, and what replaces each part

Catalogy turns PDFs and product sheets into branded interactive catalogs with quote requests, wishlists, share links and analytics. Catalog Studio covers the data, browse, compare, wishlist and quote loop in this page. The table lists open-source parts for the rest. Check each licence yourself before you ship; models on Hugging Face each carry their own.

Suggested build order

1. Use this page for your first catalogs. 2. Add Docling to convert your existing ebook and product PDFs into the CSV format on the Products tab. 3. Add PDF.js with StPageFlip for a flipbook view. 4. Move to UnoPIM when you outgrow a spreadsheet.
