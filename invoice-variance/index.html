      How Invoice Variance AI Works

      1️⃣ Upload Your Documents

      Upload Purchase Orders (what you ordered from vendors) and Invoices (what vendors billed you). Supports PDF, Excel, CSV, and photo scans.

      2️⃣ AI Agents Extract Line Items

      Multiple AI agents run in parallel. Each agent reads a document, extracts every item (SKU, description, quantity, price, unit), and normalizes the data into a standard format — even from messy scanned PDFs.

      3️⃣ Smart Comparison Engine

      Every PO line item is matched against invoice line items using fuzzy AI matching (not just exact codes). The system catches:

      • 🔴 Missing items — ordered but not on invoice
      • 🟡 Short shipments — invoiced less than ordered
      • 🔵 Price discrepancies — different price than PO
      • ⚠️ Extra items — on invoice but not on PO
      4️⃣ Export & Act

      Download a Variance Report PDF, export a CSV for your accounting system, or auto-generate a Purchase Order for missing/short items to send back to the vendor.
